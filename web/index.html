What this demonstrates

The model, pretrained policies, native controller and scene come from the public Jumper projects. ClawMetry filters the task requests in this page and shows measured simulator events. A denied request was not forwarded by this page. This does not guarantee containment or physical safety.

The seated person is a static geometric proxy. The cup and desk objects are stationary. No human detection, glass delivery, liquid handling, or autonomous tidying is demonstrated. The controller stabilizes the simulated robot; ClawMetry does not replace that controller.
